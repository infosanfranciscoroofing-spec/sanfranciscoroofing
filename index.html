How It Works

Our Roofing Process: Simple & Stress-Free

From first call to final inspection, we handle everything so you don't have to.

1

Free Inspection & Estimate

Call us or fill out the form. We schedule a free roof inspection within 2 business days. You get a detailed written report with photos and a transparent cost estimate.

2

Material Selection

We recommend materials based on your home's era, neighborhood microclimate, slope, and budget. You choose from premium options with clear cost comparisons.

3

Permits & Scheduling

We handle all building permits, HOA approvals, and Title 24 compliance. You pick the start date that works for your schedule.

4

Expert Installation

Our crew arrives on time, protects your landscaping, and completes work efficiently. Most residential jobs done in 2–3 days. Daily cleanup included.

5

Final Inspection & Warranty

City inspector sign-off, final walkthrough with you, warranty registration, and complete site cleanup. You don't pay final balance until you're 100% satisfied.
